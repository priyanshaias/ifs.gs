My younger self would have been tempted to quote Steve Jobs about dots connecting backwards and proof of a well-thought path. It's not easy to avoid the bias of retrospective predictability. But there is no pattern in the journey. Just a curious mind guided by two simple beliefs.

  • First, maximise exposure to opportunities early through stochastic tinkering. Nassim Taleb calls it seeking convexity: keep the downside finite, let the upside surprise you. The cost of trying is usually smaller than it looks.
  • Second, never take risks you can't survive. Charlie Munger kept it simpler: first, make sure you don't die!

I've tried to live by both. Things turned out better than I had any right to expect. Thank you, probability :)
