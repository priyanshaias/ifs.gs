Sources. E-way bills: GSTN e-way bill statistics by state and month, July 2018 to September 2026 (Data/GSTN Data/ewb-data-*.xlsx). GST collection and GSDP: the files used by the GST potential tool (gst-potential/data).

Notes. 2018-19 starts in July 2018, so growth is measured from 2019-20. Daman and Diu and Dadra and Nagar Haveli are combined. The source file for October 2021 has near-zero within-state and outgoing figures for ; these are replaced with the average of September and November 2021 and marked * in the month-wise table. Incoming figures for October 2021 are understated for all states for the same reason. Supplier counts are monthly figures; for a year they are the monthly average and cannot be added across movement types. "All India" is the sum of all states.
