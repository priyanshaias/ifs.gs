Capability 1 of 5 · Data integration

The foundational capability that enables the others

Capability 2 of 5 · Business logic codification and networks

Before algorithms can be applied, domain knowledge should be codified to tackle the cold-start problem

Turning intuition into algorithms shifts the work from being people-oriented to process-oriented. This reduces discretion and brings standardisation.

Capability 3 of 5 · Model training

The core engine that operates on data through business logic and learns by itself

Codification allows scalability across all taxpayers. Manual intervention through specialised cells such as RMC is more effective per case but is resource-intensive and lacks scalability.

Conversational AI is built as a stack of layers

Each layer makes the one below it safe to use on taxpayer data. Most use cases need the upper layers.

Capability 4 of 5 · Task allocation and tracking

Various actions require tracking, allocation, feedback review and revenue realisation linked with the ledger

An integrated platform will avoid duplication, allow smart allocation and feed outcome data back into the core engine for improved future predictions.

Two systems allot work to the same field officers
MyTask, 1 Apr to 31 Oct 2026. TMS charge-wise scrutiny status, 5 Nov 2026; all tax heads, as recorded. Progress is our reading.

Capability 5 of 5 · Adjudication preparedness

AI prepares drafts at each stage of adjudication while officers retain all statutory powers

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), trained on documents and specific cases, helps officers prepare notices and drafts. Every draft requires strict review by the officer. AI never exercises statutory power.
