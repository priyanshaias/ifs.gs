    What makes a case worth an officer's day

    A good case is large, proved by records someone else filed, leaves the officer little to decide, can still be collected, and is within time. Scoring every lead on these tests replaces "who shouted loudest" with one ranked list.

              The nudge test

              Nudge index = detectability × share who self-correct × amount ÷ cost per contact Send a nudge, not an officer, when detectability is 0.8 or more and the expected recovery is below what an average task returns. That bar is today.

              1. Day 0Message with the exact figure, the two returns that disagree, and a DRC-03 payment link.
              2. Day 15Reminder with a peer line ("9 in 10 dealers in your charge have matched their credit") and the interest that is running.
              3. Day 30System-drafted ASMT-10 for those above a floor; the rest stay in the next cycle's nudge.
              4. Day 60DRC-01 for the unresolved balance. Only now does the case take officer time.

              Run every message as a trial: hold back a random 10%, compare payments after 30 days, keep the wording that works. Send in Bengali, Hindi or English by taxpayer preference.
