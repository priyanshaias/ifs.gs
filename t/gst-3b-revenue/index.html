GSTR-3B comprehensive summary

GSTR-3B revenue collection

Turnover, tax liability, mode of payment and SGST revenue declared in GSTR-3B returns, by circle and charge. The main question is how much SGST revenue each formation collects for every ₹100 of turnover and taxable supplies, and why the figure differs. All amounts are in ₹ crore.

From turnover to SGST revenue

Each step of the funnel is one ratio. Multiplied together they give SGST revenue per ₹100 of turnover. This shows whether a low collection comes from a small taxable base, a low tax rate on that base or a small share of tax reaching the state as SGST.

The three ratios

Selected scope in large type. For comparison: the state figure and the median charge (field charges, LTU left out). Medians do not multiply exactly.

SGST revenue as a share of tax liability is low by design: CGST goes to the Centre and IGST is settled between states. It rises when more tax is paid in cash and falls when IGST is paid out of SGST credit.

How SGST revenue is built

The report's "Total SGST revenue" adds SGST paid in cash (with interest and late fee) to the IGST credit settled to the state and subtracts IGST paid out of SGST credit.

    The report's "Total SGST revenue as per GSTR-3B": SGST cash, interest and late fee + SGST paid through IGST ITC + 50% of IGST ITC reversed under 4(B)(1) + 50% of ineligible IGST ITC under 4(D)(2) − IGST paid through SGST ITC.
