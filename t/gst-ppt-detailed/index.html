        Understanding technology in GST

        GST administration has an inherent edge in being digital from the source

        Unlike physical-world government departments where operations occur on physical ground (such as roads constructed on site or physical scene investigations in criminal justice), GST transactions originate natively in digital, structured datasets. However, this shifts value generation to algorithms.

        Understanding technology in GST

        Every evasion is a data problem first, field work then confirms and recovers

        Every evasion leaves a gap three witnesses can see. An evader controls only their own return. The counterparty, the physical and money trail and normal behaviour all record what really happened.

        Every return must agree with three independent witnesses. Each needs a different level of analytical effort to check.

        Visible tools and targeted actions are low-hanging fruit and should be pursued first. Sustained value comes from the invisible layer underneath: the continuous process that builds, tests and refreshes rules as evaders adapt.

            Annex A3

            The three measures and data sources

            Gross collection − CGST − IGST = Own SGST  ·  Own SGST + IGST settlement = SGST after settlementAll three are for taxpayers registered in the State, 2025-26, before refunds.

            1 · Gross collection
            CGST, SGST and IGST paid by taxpayers registered in the State on domestic supplies. Cess and IGST on imports are not included.
            2 · Own SGST
            SGST paid by taxpayers registered in the State on supplies within the State. It is the State’s revenue before IGST settlement.
            3 · SGST after settlement
            Own SGST plus the State’s share of IGST settled by the Centre. IGST is settled to the State where the goods or services are consumed, so a consuming State receives more than the IGST its taxpayers pay.
            Active taxpayers
            Registrations as on 30 June 2026, net of cancellation and revocation with retrospective effect. Normal taxpayers, composition taxpayers, input service distributors (ISD), tax collectors (TCS), tax deductors (TDS) and others.
            Major states
            The eleven States with the most active taxpayers: Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Rajasthan, Delhi, West Bengal, Bihar, Haryana and Madhya Pradesh.
            Sources
            GSTN GST statistics: state-wise GST collection, settlement of IGST to States (regular settlement; there was no ad hoc settlement in 2025-26) and registration statistics.

            Annex A6

            Five immediate steps and measuring the invisibles

            None of this needs a large new IT procurement. The invisibles can be set up at once, alongside quick visible wins.

            Five immediate steps
            1. 1

              Launch a rule library

              Encode the core mismatch rules and the first three EIU and RMC cases with formal written specifications.

            2. 2

              Name Link Officers

              Identify a small group of officers with strong field instinct and analytical clarity to serve as domain-to-code translators.

            3. 3

              Activate one pooled queue

              Merge flags at the GSTIN level, score them together and allocate within each charge’s real capacity.

            4. 4

              Mandate structured closure outcomes

              Record proved demand, cash recovered and the reason for closure in standard fields on every disposal.

            5. 5

              Hold a monthly Rule Council

              Turn field intelligence into a steady monthly pipeline of live one-click rules.

            Measuring the invisiblesbeyond the count of cases disposed
            MeasureWhat it tells leadership
            Revenue realised per officer-hourWhether scarce officer capacity is reaching the highest-yield cases
            Precision of each live ruleWhether flags are real evasion and honest taxpayers are left undisturbed
            Share of notices dropped without demandThe compliance burden falling on honest businesses (a check on Ease of Doing Business)
            Time from field insight to live ruleHow fast the institution learns and counters evaders as they adapt
            Live rules in production and their combined yieldWhether institutional knowledge is compounding cycle after cycle
